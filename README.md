# RUN

**Run** is a game about escaping an airport while a mad man with a lit match tries to explode you after strapping a bomb to you. Survive 3 levels of dodging obstacles to win.

## Story

You fell asleep in a chair at the airport...

You are awakened by a man strapping a band of explosives around you!

He smiles at you, then lights a match...

Run.

## Controls:

**Mobile:** Use on screen controls

**Desktop:** Arrow keys to select menus, 'z' or 'c' or 'n' for O button, 'x' or 'v' or 'm' for X button.
